Keeping your spine straight and shoulders relaxed, glide your left leg backward to the other end of the board while bending your right knee to maintain balance. Now, press through your right foot to slide the carriage forward while lifting your left knee toward your chest until it reaches waist height, bringing your arms down in front of you simultaneously. Hold briefly, then slowly extend the leg to achieve the initial position. Repeat for the specified reps on both sides.
ROLL DOWN
total body,10,3,hard
Stand one hand's distance away from the Pilates Board, facing it, with feet hip-width apart. Place both hands on the moving carriage positioned at the near end of the board, keeping your knees straight and heels slightly lifted off the ground. Keeping your heels lifted and knees straight, glide the moving carriage forward in a controlled motion toward the far end of the board, maintaining straight elbows and a stable core. Hold briefly at the extended position, engaging your core and shoulders, then slowly glide the carriage back to return to the starting position. Repeat for the recommended number of repetitions.
GLIDE KICKBACK
total body,10,3,hard
Kneel beside the Pilates Board in a tabletop position, resting your forearms on the moving carriage positioned at the closer end of the board with elbows aligned directly under your shoulders. Keeping your upper body stable and holding the carriage handles firmly, draw your left knee in toward your chest in a smooth, controlled motion. Then, extend your left leg up and backward until it is fully straight and slightly above glute level while pushing the moving carriage towards the centre simultaneously. Hold briefly at the extended position, then slowly lower down the leg to achieve the initial tabletop position. Repeat for the recommended number of repetitions, on both the sides.
